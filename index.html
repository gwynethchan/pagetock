Before you turn the page

A few things to know.

Is PageTock an ebook reader?

PageTock is a reading companion designed with physical books in mind. It helps you make time to read and track your progress; it doesn’t contain ebooks.

Can I track the pages I read?

Yes. Log the page you reached after a reading session, so you can keep track of your progress through each book.

Does PageTock block other apps?

No. PageTock encourages focus with an on-screen timer. Leaving the app pauses your reading time and starts a five-minute grace period. You stay in control of your phone.

Where can I download PageTock?

PageTock is now available for iPhone on the US App Store. Download PageTock and start your next reading session.
